CodexHost includes a standalone **Web app** that connects to the exact same background Host as Codex Desktop while **using noticeably less memory**.

As long as your computer stays awake and online, you can monitor tasks, approve tool calls, and chat with every installed Agent from your phone, tablet, or any browser.

## Step 1: Start the Web App on Your Computer

```bash
codexhost web
```

This starts the Web service in the background (listening locally on `http://127.0.0.1:4400` by default) and opens your browser.

> **Safe to close the tab**: Closing your terminal window or browser tab will **not** stop the Web server or interrupt running Agent tasks. Run `codexhost stop` only when you want to shut everything down.

---

## Step 2: Connect Your Phone or Another Device

On your computer, click **Remote Access** in the Web sidebar (or open **Settings → Connected Devices**), choose a connection method, and scan the QR code with your phone camera:

| Connection Method                          | Best When…                                                                   |               Encrypted?                | What You Need                                                                       |
| :----------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Official Encrypted Relay (Recommended)** | You're away from home/office and don't want to install any app on your phone | HTTPS (decrypted only on your computer) | Nothing—just click **Enable Relay**                                                 |
| **Tailscale HTTPS (Recommended)**          | You want private cross-network access restricted to your own devices         |                  HTTPS                  | Tailscale installed on both computer and phone                                      |
| **Same Wi-Fi**                             | Quick local access at home or in a trusted office                            |               Plain HTTP                | Phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi                                                |
| **Self-Hosted Relay / Custom Tunnel**      | You own a server or domain and want full control over routing                |                  HTTPS                  | See [Self-Hosted Relay](/en/docs/web/relay), or use Cloudflare Tunnel / nginx / frp |

> **Treat QR codes like passwords**: Both the QR code (blurred on screen until clicked) and the copied login link contain your **access token**—anyone holding it can drive Agents on your computer. Never share them publicly, and never expose `--no-auth` on a network.

## Handy Web & Mobile Features

- **Home-screen PWA & Push Notifications**: Add the Web app to your phone's home screen, then enable push alerts under **Settings → General → App & Notifications** to get notified whenever a turn finishes or an Agent needs your approval.
- **Delegation Session Tree**: Child tasks spawned with `#` nest neatly underneath their parent session in the sidebar.
- **Select Text to Quote**: Highlight any part of an Agent's reply to quote it straight into your next message.
- **On-Demand Desktop Screenshot & Browser Stream**: Away from your desk? Grab a live screenshot of your Windows/macOS desktop on demand, or stream a server-side browser right inside the Web sidebar.
- **Web UI Extensions**: Install, enable, and manage custom UI extension plugins.
