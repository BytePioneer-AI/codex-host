## Do I Need to Self-Host a Relay?

For most people, **no**:

- If you just want to reach your computer from your phone while away, turn on the **Official Encrypted Relay** in **Settings → Connected Devices**, or use **Tailscale**.
- Self-hosting is for when **you already own a domain and a public Linux VPS** and want your own custom URL (like `https://my-mac.example.com`) on infrastructure you control.

### How Does It Stay Private?

The Relay server **only forwards raw encrypted bytes**. Your home/office computer requests its own Let's Encrypt TLS certificate and decrypts HTTPS traffic locally—**the Relay server itself never holds your private key and cannot read your chats or code.**

---

## What You Need Before Starting

1. **A Linux server with a public IPv4 address**: Git and Docker (with Docker Compose) installed, and **TCP port 443** open in your firewall / cloud security group (port 80 is not needed, and nothing else should be bound to 443).
2. **Your own domain** with a wildcard DNS record:
   - **Type**: `A`
   - **Name / Host**: `*` (wildcard—existing `www` or root `@` records keep working untouched)
   - **Value**: Your server's public IPv4 address (if you use Cloudflare DNS, set proxy status to **DNS only / grey cloud**)

## Deploy & Connect in 2 Steps

### Step 1: Start the Relay on Your Server

SSH into your server, replace `example.com` below with your actual domain, and run:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/BytePioneer-AI/codex-host.git /opt/codexhost
cd /opt/codexhost/packages/remote-relay/deploy
echo "RELAY_DOMAIN=example.com" > .env
docker compose up -d --build
```

Compose builds directly from source (no Node.js needed on the host) and automatically manages the control-plane certificate on port 443.

### Step 2: Enable It on Your Computer

1. Open CodexHost Web on your computer (`codexhost web`) and go to **Settings → Connected Devices**.
2. Select **Self-Hosted Encrypted Relay**.
3. Enter your **Relay domain** (e.g. `example.com`, without `https://` or `relay.`) and a **Device name** (e.g. `my-mac`).
4. Click **Enable Relay**. Within about a minute your computer will provision its certificate, and you can scan the QR code on your phone to connect via `https://my-mac.example.com`.

> For rate limits, custom certificates, and troubleshooting tips, see the [full Self-Hosted Relay guide](https://github.com/BytePioneer-AI/codex-host/blob/main/docs/product/self-hosted-relay.md) in the repository.
